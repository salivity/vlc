Prevent VLC from Disabling Screensaver and Sleep

By default, VLC Media Player overrides your operating system's power management settings to prevent the screen from turning off or the computer from sleeping while a video is playing. While convenient for uninterrupted movie watching, this feature can lead to unwanted battery drain or screen burn-in if left running unattended. You can easily adjust VLC's internal preferences to stop it from blocking your screensaver and system sleep timers.

Method 1: Disable via Simple Video Settings

The quickest way to allow your screensaver to trigger during playback is through the main video preferences menu:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. Select the Video tab at the top.
  4. Locate the option labeled Disable screensaver and uncheck the box.
  5. Click Save at the bottom of the window.
  6. Restart VLC for the changes to take full effect.

Method 2: Disable Power Management Inhibition (Advanced Settings)

If your computer still refuses to sleep after disabling the screensaver option, you must turn off VLC's power management inhibitor in the advanced settings:

  1. Open VLC Media Player and go to Tools > Preferences.
  2. At the bottom-left corner under Show settings, select the All radio button to access advanced options.
  3. In the left sidebar, expand or click on Advanced.
  4. In the right pane, find the option labeled Inhibit the power management daemon during playback.
  5. Uncheck this option to stop VLC from telling your operating system to stay awake.
  6. Click Save and restart VLC.

Once these options are disabled, your operating system will handle idle times according to its standard power plan, activating your screensaver or entering sleep mode even while media is loaded in VLC.
