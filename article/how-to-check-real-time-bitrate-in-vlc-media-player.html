How to Check Real-Time Bitrate in VLC Media Player

Monitoring your video's bitrate helps evaluate playback performance, network streaming efficiency, and overall file quality. VLC Media Player features a built-in statistical panel that tracks real-time input and demuxing bitrates for both audio and video streams without requiring third-party plugins. This guide explains how to open the statistics window and interpret the live data while a video is playing.

Step 1: Play Your Video

Open VLC Media Player and start playing the video file or network stream you want to monitor.

Step 2: Open Media Information

With the video active, open the media properties window using either of the following methods:

  • Menu Bar: Click on Tools in the top menu and select Media Information.
  • Keyboard Shortcut: Press Ctrl + I on Windows and Linux, or Cmd + I on macOS.

Step 3: Switch to the Statistics Tab

In the window that appears, click on the Statistics tab located at the top right.

Step 4: Monitor the Bitrate in Real-Time

Under the Input/Read section, you will see real-time statistics updating every second as the video plays:

  • Input bitrate: Displays the overall incoming data rate (audio, video, and container overhead combined) in kb/s.
  • Demuxed bitrate: Shows the rate at which VLC processes and splits the media stream into distinct audio and video tracks.
  • Bitrate Graph: A dynamic line graph provides a visual representation of bitrate spikes and drops throughout playback.

Keep this window open side-by-side with your playback window to monitor performance fluctuations continuously.
