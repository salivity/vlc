How to Change Subtitle Outline Thickness in VLC

Adjusting the subtitle outline thickness in VLC Media Player helps improve readability against bright or busy video backgrounds. This guide walks you through the exact steps to access VLC's advanced preferences, locate the text renderer options, and adjust the outline thickness to suit your viewing needs.

Step 1: Open the Preferences Menu

Launch VLC Media Player. In the top menu bar, click on Tools and select Preferences from the drop-down menu. Alternatively, you can press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux or Cmd + , on macOS.

Step 2: Switch to Advanced Settings

By default, VLC opens in the "Simple" preferences view, which does not display the outline thickness option.

  1. Look at the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window under the Show settings section.
  2. Select the All radio button to switch to the advanced settings view.

Step 3: Navigate to Text Renderer Settings

  1. In the left-hand sidebar, scroll down to the Video category.
  2. Expand the Subtitles / OSD section by clicking the arrow or plus icon next to it.
  3. Click directly on Text renderer (in some VLC versions, this may be labeled as Freetype2 font renderer).

Step 4: Adjust the Outline Thickness

  1. On the right-hand panel, locate the Outline thickness setting.
  2. Use the drop-down menu to select your preferred thickness. The available options typically include:
    • None
    • Thin
    • Normal
    • Thick
    • Very thick
  3. Select the option that best fits your preference.

Step 5: Save and Restart VLC

  1. Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner of the window.
  2. Close and restart VLC Media Player for the changes to take effect.

The next time you play a video with subtitles, the text will render with your selected outline thickness.
