How to Cast VLC to Amazon Fire TV Stick

Streaming videos and audio from VLC Media Player to an Amazon Fire TV Stick allows you to enjoy locally stored media on a larger screen without complicated setups. Because the Fire TV platform does not natively support Google Cast—the primary protocol VLC uses for its built-in casting feature—the best ways to achieve this are by using a casting receiver app on your Fire TV, mirroring your display directly from your device, or accessing shared network media directly through the native Fire TV VLC application.

Method 1: Using VLC's Built-in Renderer with a Receiver App

To use VLC’s native "Renderer" tool to stream content from a PC or Mac, you must first enable Google Cast support on your Fire TV using a receiver app such as AirScreen.

  1. Set Up the Receiver:
    • On your Fire TV, search for and install AirScreen from the Amazon Appstore.
    • Launch AirScreen, follow the initial setup prompts, and ensure the status shows that the server is running.
  2. Connect Devices:
    • Ensure your computer and your Fire TV Stick are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Cast from VLC:
    • Open VLC Media Player on your computer.
    • Click on Playback in the top menu bar.
    • Hover over Renderer.
    • Select your Fire TV Stick (or the device name displayed by the receiver app) from the list.
    • Open the media file you want to play. VLC will now stream the video and audio directly to your television.

Method 2: Display Mirroring (Windows and Android)

If you do not want to install extra receiver software, you can mirror your screen directly while playing media in VLC.

From Windows 10/11:

  1. On your Fire TV remote, press and hold the Home button and select Mirroring.
  2. On your Windows PC, press Windows Key + K to open the Cast/Connect menu.
  3. Click on your Fire TV Stick to connect.
  4. Open VLC on your PC, start your media, and switch to full-screen mode.

From Android:

  1. Enable Mirroring on the Fire TV Stick via the Home button menu.
  2. On your Android device, swipe down the notification shade and tap Cast, Smart View, or Screen Mirroring.
  3. Select your Fire TV Stick from the list of devices.
  4. Launch the VLC app on Android, play your video, and rotate to landscape mode.

Method 3: Playing Shared PC Media on Fire TV's VLC App

Instead of pushing content from a computer, you can pull media over your local network using the native Fire TV VLC app, which avoids latency and transcoding issues.

  1. Share Media on Your Computer:
    • Put your media files in a folder on your PC or Mac.
    • Right-click the folder, access its sharing properties, and share it over the local network via SMB or Windows File Sharing.
  2. Install VLC on Fire TV:
    • Search for and install VLC for Fire via the Fire TV Appstore.
  3. Play Network Media:
    • Open VLC on your Fire TV Stick.
    • Navigate to the Browsing tab and select Local Network.
    • Locate your computer name, enter any required login credentials, and browse your folders to stream files directly.
