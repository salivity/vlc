How to Adjust Video Brightness and Contrast in VLC

VLC Media Player allows users to dynamically modify video parameters such as brightness, contrast, saturation, and gamma in real time while media is playing. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to locate the built-in video effects panel, enable the image adjustment controls, and fine-tune your display settings without modifying the original video file.

Step 1: Open the Adjustments and Effects Menu

  1. Open your video file in VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on the Tools menu on the top menu bar.
  3. Select Effects and Filters from the dropdown menu. (Alternatively, press Ctrl + E on Windows/Linux or Cmd + E on macOS).

Step 2: Enable Image Adjustments

  1. In the Adjustments and Effects dialog box, select the Video Effects tab.
  2. Under the Essential sub-tab, locate and check the box labeled Image adjust.
  3. Once enabled, the adjustment sliders will become active.

Step 3: Adjust Brightness and Contrast

  • Brightness: Drag the slider to the right to brighten dark footage, or to the left to reduce overall luminance.
  • Contrast: Drag the slider to the right to widen the difference between light and dark areas, making the picture sharper, or to the left to flatten the tones.

Changes apply immediately in the background window, allowing you to monitor the visual results dynamically as the video continues to play.

Step 4: Retaining or Resetting Settings

  • Temporary Adjustments: If you only need these settings for the current viewing session, simply close the Adjustments and Effects window. When VLC is closed and reopened, default settings typically apply unless configured otherwise.
  • Resetting: To restore original video levels at any point, uncheck the Image adjust box or drag the sliders back to their centered default positions.
  • Persistent Settings (Optional): To keep these settings for every video played in the future, open Tools > Preferences (Ctrl + P), select All under Show settings at the bottom left, navigate to Video > Filters, check Image properties filter, and click Save.
