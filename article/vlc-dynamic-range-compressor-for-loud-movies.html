VLC Dynamic Range Compressor for Loud Movies

VLC Media Player includes a built-in dynamic range compressor designed to balance audio levels, making it ideal for watching action movies with quiet dialogue and deafening sound effects. This article explains how to locate the tool, enable it, and configure the optimal settings so you no longer have to manually adjust your volume during playback.

How to Access and Enable the Compressor

VLC's compressor is located within its audio effects menu. You can enable it using the following steps:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Navigate to Tools in the top menu bar and select Effects and Filters (or press Ctrl + E on Windows / Cmd + E on Mac).
  3. In the "Adjustments and Effects" window, click the Audio Effects tab.
  4. Select the Compressor tab.
  5. Check the box labeled Enable dynamic range compressor (or simply Enable depending on your VLC version).

Optimal Compressor Settings for Action Movies

Once enabled, several sliders become available. To tame loud explosions while boosting quiet dialogue, adjust the sliders to the following baseline values:

  • RMS/Peak: Set toward 0.00 (Peak). This ensures the compressor reacts immediately to sudden spikes in volume.
  • Attack Time: Set between 1.5 ms and 10 ms. A fast attack time clamps down on sudden loud sounds like explosions or gunshots instantly.
  • Release Time: Set around 100 ms to 300 ms. This smoothly restores normal volume after a loud sound finishes, preventing an unnatural "pumping" effect.
  • Threshold: Set to roughly -15 dB to -20 dB. Any sound louder than this threshold will be compressed. If the audio is still too dynamic, lower the threshold further.
  • Ratio: Set to 4:1 or 8:1. This determines how heavily the volume above the threshold is reduced. A higher ratio creates a flatter, more consistent sound level.
  • Knee Radius: Set to 2.5 dB to 5.0 dB. This softens the transition into compression for a more natural audio curve.
  • Makeup Gain: Set between 6 dB and 10 dB. Compressing the peaks reduces overall volume; makeup gain boosts the entire audio track so quiet dialogue becomes clear and audible.

Making the Settings Permanent

By default, VLC resets audio effects when closed. To save your compressor settings for all future playback:

  1. Go to Tools > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  2. At the bottom left under "Show settings," select All.
  3. In the left sidebar, expand Audio and click Filters.
  4. Check the box for Dynamic range compressor.
  5. In the left panel, expand Filters under Audio and click Compressor to input your preferred default slider values.
  6. Click Save and restart VLC.
