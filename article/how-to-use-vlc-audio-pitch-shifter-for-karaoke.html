How to Use VLC Audio Pitch Shifter for Karaoke

VLC Media Player includes built-in audio processing filters capable of shifting pitch, making it an effective, free utility for karaoke performers needing to transpose a song's key to match their vocal range. This article explains how VLC handles audio pitch shifting for karaoke purposes, details how to activate and configure the filter, and highlights additional vocal reduction features built into the player.

Does VLC Support Pitch Shifting?

Yes. VLC Media Player features a real-time audio pitch shifter that alters the musical key of an audio or video file without changing the playback speed. This allows singers to practice or perform tracks that are originally too high or too low for their natural vocal register.

How to Enable the Pitch Shifter in VLC

To adjust the pitch during media playback, follow these steps:

  1. Open VLC Media Player and load your karaoke audio or video file.
  2. Go to the top menu bar and select Tools, then click on Effects and Filters (or use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + E on Windows/Linux, or Cmd + E on macOS).
  3. In the "Adjustments and Effects" window, switch to the Audio Effects tab.
  4. Click on the Pitch tab.
  5. Check the box to enable the pitch shift module.
  6. Adjust the slider left to lower the key (flat) or right to raise the key (sharp) by semitones until the track fits your voice.

Changes apply in real time, so you can adjust the key while the track plays to find the optimal pitch.

Using VLC's Dedicated Karaoke Filter

In addition to pitch shifting, VLC features a native "Karaoke" filter designed to eliminate or attenuate lead vocals from stereo tracks:

  1. Open Tools > Effects and Filters.
  2. Select the Audio Effects tab, then locate the Advanced or Karaoke tab (depending on your VLC version).
  3. Check the Karaoke option.

This filter works by inverting stereo phases to cancel out center-panned audio, which is where lead vocals are typically mixed. While its effectiveness depends on how the original track was mastered, pairing this feature with the pitch shifter turns standard audio files into adaptable karaoke accompaniment tracks.
