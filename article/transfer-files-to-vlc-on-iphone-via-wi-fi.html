Transfer Files to VLC on iPhone via Wi-Fi

Transferring media files to the VLC app on an iPhone or iPad via Wi-Fi sharing is a simple, cable-free process that works across any operating system. By enabling VLC's built-in local web server, you can upload video, audio, and subtitle files directly from a desktop or laptop web browser to your iOS device without using iTunes, Finder, or cloud storage.

Prerequisites

  • Ensure both your iOS device and your computer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.
  • Ensure you have the VLC app installed on your iPhone or iPad.

Step 1: Enable Wi-Fi Sharing in VLC

  1. Open the VLC app on your iOS device.
  2. Tap the Network tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Locate the Sharing via Wi-Fi option and toggle the switch to the On position.
  4. Once toggled on, an IP address (e.g., http://192.168.1.45) and a local address name (e.g., http://your-iphone.local) will appear directly beneath the toggle. Keep this screen open on your device.

Step 2: Open the VLC Web Interface on Your Computer

  1. Open any modern web browser (such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge) on your computer.
  2. Type either the IP address or the .local address displayed in the VLC app into the browser's address bar and press Enter.
  3. A webpage titled "Sharing via Wi-Fi" will load, displaying an upload interface.

Step 3: Upload Your Media Files

  1. Drag and drop your audio or video files directly into the browser window.
  2. Alternatively, click the + (plus) icon in the top-right corner of the web page to select files manually from your file explorer.
  3. Wait for the upload progress bar to complete for each file.

Step 4: Access Files on Your iOS Device

Once the progress bar reaches 100%, the media files are stored locally on your device. Return to your iOS device, tap the Video or Audio tab in the VLC app, and your newly transferred media will be ready to play immediately. You can now toggle off Sharing via Wi-Fi to save battery life.
