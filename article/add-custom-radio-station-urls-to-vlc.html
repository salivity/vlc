Add Custom Radio Station URLs to VLC

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on how to manually add and permanently save custom online radio station URLs to VLC Media Player. By following these steps, you will learn how to locate a stream link, open it within VLC, and store it directly in your VLC Media Library or playlist for quick, ongoing access without relying on third-party directories.

Step 1: Find the Direct Radio Stream URL

Before opening VLC, you need the direct streaming link of the radio station. Common audio stream formats include URLs ending in .m3u, .pls, .aac, or direct HTTP/HTTPS audio streams (such as Icecast or Shoutcast links). You can usually find these links on the station's official website under their "Listen Live" or "Stream Links" section.

Step 2: Open the Network Stream in VLC

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on the Media menu in the top toolbar (or File on macOS).
  3. Select Open Network Stream... (or press the shortcut Ctrl + N on Windows/Linux, Cmd + N on macOS).

Step 3: Enter the Radio Station URL

  1. In the Open Media window, select the Network tab.
  2. In the field labeled Please enter a network URL, paste the URL of the radio station.
  3. Click the Play button at the bottom. The audio should begin buffering and playing automatically.

Step 4: Save the Station to the VLC Media Library

By default, closing VLC will clear the current stream. To keep the radio station accessible permanently in your VLC directory:

  1. Open the playlist view by clicking View > Playlist (or press Ctrl + L / Cmd + L).
  2. Look at the left sidebar and locate Media Library.
  3. In the playlist area, find the currently playing radio station.
  4. Click and drag the radio stream entry from the playlist pane onto Media Library in the left sidebar.

Alternatively, you can save your radio collection as a local playlist file:

  1. Go to Media > Save Playlist to File...
  2. Choose a format like M3U Playlist (*.m3u), name your file, and save it to your computer.
  3. You can reopen this file in VLC at any time to access your saved custom stations.
