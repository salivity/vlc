How to Add a Custom Logo Overlay in VLC

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to permanently display a custom logo or watermark overlay on all videos played in VLC Media Player. By configuring the logo video filter within VLC's advanced preferences, you can ensure that your chosen image automatically appears in a fixed position whenever media is played, eliminating the need to re-apply the overlay for each individual session.

1. Prepare Your Image

Before configuring VLC, make sure your logo image is ready:

  • Format: PNG format is recommended because it supports transparency, allowing your logo to blend cleanly with the video background.
  • Storage Location: Move the image file to a permanent location on your computer where it will not be moved or deleted accidentally (e.g., C:\VLC\logo.png).

2. Open Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. In the top menu, go to Tools and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  3. In the bottom-left corner under the Show settings section, toggle the option from Simple to All. This displays the advanced configuration menu.

3. Enable the Logo Video Filter

  1. In the left-hand navigation pane, scroll down and expand the Video category.
  2. Click on Filters.
  3. On the right-hand panel, find the list of video filters and check the box next to Logo video filter.

4. Configure the Logo Settings

  1. In the left-hand panel, expand Filters under the Video section (or expand Subtitles / OSD depending on your VLC version).
  2. Click directly on Logo overlay.
  3. Configure the following parameters:
    • Logo full path name: Enter the complete file path to your image (e.g., C:\VLC\logo.png).
    • X coordinate: Set the horizontal position in pixels from the left edge of the video frame.
    • Y coordinate: Set the vertical position in pixels from the top edge of the video frame.
    • Logo opacity: Adjust the transparency of the logo, ranging from 0 (completely invisible) to 255 (fully opaque).
    • Logo position: Alternatively, use the drop-down menu to set relative alignment (e.g., Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Center).

5. Save and Restart VLC

  1. Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner of the Preferences window.
  2. Close VLC completely to ensure the changes take effect.
  3. Open any video file with VLC. Your custom logo overlay will now appear automatically in the designated position across all playback sessions.
