How to Change VLC Jump and Skip Times

This article provides a step-by-step guide on how to customize the default jump lengths for shortcut skips in VLC Media Player. By modifying VLC's advanced preferences, you can adjust the exact duration in seconds for very short, short, medium, and long skips to tailor media playback navigation to your preferences.

Default VLC Jump Shortcuts

By default, VLC assigns the following keyboard shortcuts and durations:

  • Very short jump: Shift + Left/Right Arrow (Default: 3 seconds)
  • Short jump: Alt + Left/Right Arrow (Default: 10 seconds)
  • Medium jump: Ctrl + Left/Right Arrow (Default: 1 minute)
  • Long jump: Ctrl + Alt + Left/Right Arrow (Default: 5 minutes)

(Note: On macOS, use Cmd instead of Ctrl and Option instead of Alt.)

Steps to Change Jump Times in VLC

  1. Open Preferences: Launch VLC Media Player. In the top menu bar, click on Tools and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).

  2. Switch to Advanced Preferences: In the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, locate the Show settings section. Switch the selection from Simple to All.

  3. Navigate to Hotkeys Settings: In the left sidebar list, expand the Interface category and select Hotkeys settings.

  4. Adjust the Jump Lengths: In the main pane on the right, scroll until you locate the Jump sizes section. You will see four customizable fields:

    • Very short jump length
    • Short jump length
    • Medium jump length
    • Long jump length

    Enter your desired skip duration (in seconds) into any of the corresponding fields.

  5. Save and Restart: Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner. Restart VLC Media Player for the new jump intervals to take effect.
