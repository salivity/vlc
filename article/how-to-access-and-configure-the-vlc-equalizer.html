How to Access and Configure the VLC Equalizer

VLC Media Player includes a built-in 10-band graphic equalizer that allows you to fine-tune your audio output for movies and music. This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to locate, enable, and adjust the equalizer on desktop systems, choose pre-configured sound profiles, and manually balance frequencies to achieve optimal audio quality.

How to Access the Equalizer in VLC

You can access the equalizer through the menu bar or via keyboard shortcuts depending on your operating system:

  • Windows and Linux: Click Tools in the top menu bar, then select Effects and Filters. Alternatively, press Ctrl + E on your keyboard.
  • macOS: Click Window in the top menu bar, then select Audio Effects. Alternatively, press Cmd + E on your keyboard.

How to Enable and Configure the Equalizer

Once the adjustments window is open, configure the audio settings using the following steps:

  1. Select the Tab: Click on the Audio Effects tab, then select the Equalizer sub-tab.
  2. Turn On the Equalizer: Check the Enable box. The sliders will change from grayed-out to active.
  3. Choose a Preset (Optional): Click the Preset drop-down menu to choose an audio profile tailored for specific genres or hardware, such as Rock, Pop, Classical, Full Bass, or Headphones.
  4. Manually Adjust Sliders:
    • Preamp: Located on the far left, this slider raises or lowers the overall volume level of the track before applying frequency adjustments. Lower this if you hear audio distortion or clipping.
    • Frequency Bands: The ten vertical sliders range from 60 Hz (deep bass) to 16 kHz (high treble). Move sliders upward to boost specific frequencies or downward to attenuate them.
  5. Enable 2-Pass: Check the 2-Pass option if you want VLC to process the audio filter twice, resulting in a cleaner, more precise frequency curve with reduced distortion.

How to Make Equalizer Settings Permanent

By default, VLC may revert to a flat profile when restarted. To apply an equalizer preset permanently across all sessions:

  1. Go to Tools > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P / Cmd + ,).
  2. At the bottom left under Show settings, select All.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Audio > Filters.
  4. Check the box for Equalizer with preset.
  5. Expand Filters in the sidebar, select Equalizer, choose your preferred preset from the drop-down menu, and click Save.
