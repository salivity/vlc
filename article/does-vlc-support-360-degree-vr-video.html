Does VLC Support 360-Degree VR Video?

VLC Media Player fully supports 360-degree virtual reality (VR) video playback, allowing users to watch and navigate immersive spherical content directly on their desktops. This feature, introduced natively in VLC 3.0 (codenamed "Vetinari"), enables pan-and-tilt control over the viewing angle using a mouse or keyboard. The following sections explore how this feature works, its system compatibility, and how to control 360-degree videos within the media player.

Understanding VLC's 360-Degree Video Support

VLC achieved full 360-degree capability through a partnership with Giroptic, a prominent developer of 360-degree cameras. The media player can read spatial metadata embedded within 360-degree video files and photos, rendering them as interactive spheres rather than flat, distorted panoramas.

Key Features of VLC's VR Playback

  • Format Compatibility: VLC supports major spherical video formats, including equirectangular projections and standard 360-degree spatial metadata.
  • Interactive Controls: Users can click and drag the mouse inside the video window to look around in any direction, or use the keyboard arrow keys to pan left, right, up, and down.
  • Zoom Functionality: The scroll wheel on a mouse allows users to zoom in and out of the spherical environment seamlessly.
  • 360-Degree Spatial Audio: In addition to visual immersion, VLC supports spatial audio formats, meaning the audio direction shifts dynamically as the user pans through the video scene.

Supported Formats and Devices

VLC handles high-resolution 360-degree footage, including \(4\text{K}\) and \(8\text{K}\) resolutions, provided the host computer has sufficient hardware acceleration capabilities.

Platform Availability

The 360-degree playback feature is natively built into the desktop versions of VLC for Windows and macOS. While the core desktop application allows for interactive screen panning, full integration with dedicated virtual reality headsets (such as Meta Quest or HTC Vive) requires specific VLC VR builds or plugins, as the standard desktop version is primarily optimized for monitor-based navigation.

How to Play 360-Degree Videos in VLC

Playing a 360-degree video requires no special configuration or third-party extensions. The process is identical to playing a standard video file.

  1. Launch the latest version of VLC Media Player on your computer.
  2. Open the 360-degree video file by dragging it into the VLC window or navigating to Media > Open File.
  3. Once the video begins playing, left-click anywhere on the video screen and drag the cursor to change the camera angle.
