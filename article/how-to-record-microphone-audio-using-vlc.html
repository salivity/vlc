How to Record Microphone Audio Using VLC

VLC Media Player is widely known for playing video and audio files, but it also includes built-in tools for capturing live audio. This guide explains how to configure VLC to detect your computer's microphone, set up the output format, and record high-quality audio files directly to your local storage without needing additional recording software.

Step 1: Open the Capture Device Menu

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Media in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Open Capture Device... from the dropdown menu (or press Ctrl + C on Windows).

Step 2: Configure Your Audio Input

  1. In the Capture Device tab, ensure the Capture mode dropdown is set to DirectShow.
  2. Locate the Video device name field and set it to None.
  3. Locate the Audio device name field and select your connected microphone from the dropdown list.

Step 3: Set Output Settings and File Destination

  1. Look at the bottom-right corner of the window next to the Play button. Click the small downward arrow attached to it.
  2. Select Convert from the list (or press Alt + O).
  3. In the Convert window, find the Profile dropdown under the Settings section and choose your preferred audio format (such as Audio - MP3 or Audio - FLAC).
  4. Click the Browse button next to Destination file to choose where the recording will be saved.
  5. Enter a name for your file, ensure the correct file extension is added (for example, my_recording.mp3), and click Save.

Step 4: Record and Save

  1. Click the Start button to begin recording. VLC will immediately start capturing input from your microphone.
  2. The VLC playback slider will move forward, indicating the elapsed recording time.
  3. Once you finish speaking, click the square Stop button on the VLC control bar to end the recording.

Your audio file is now finalized and saved in the destination folder you selected.
