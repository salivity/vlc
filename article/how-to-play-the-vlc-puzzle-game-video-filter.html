How to Play the VLC Puzzle Game Video Filter

VLC Media Player includes a hidden interactive feature that transforms any playing video into a real-time jigsaw puzzle. This guide explains how to locate the Puzzle game video filter within VLC's settings, configure the grid dimensions to adjust the difficulty, and solve the puzzle while your media continues to play in the background.

Step 1: Open Your Video in VLC

Launch VLC Media Player and open the video file you want to use. Start playback so you can see the video actively running.

Step 2: Open Effects and Filters

Navigate to the top menu bar and click on Tools, then select Effects and Filters from the dropdown menu. Alternatively, press Ctrl + E on Windows/Linux or Cmd + E on macOS to open the adjustments panel.

Step 3: Locate the Geometry Settings

  1. In the "Adjustments and Effects" window, select the Video Effects tab.
  2. Underneath the main tabs, scroll horizontally through the sub-tabs and click on Geometry.

Step 4: Enable and Configure the Puzzle Game

  1. Locate the Puzzle game option and check the box next to it.
  2. The video display will instantly fracture into a scrambled grid of tiles.
  3. Adjust the Rows and Columns counters to set the grid size. For example, a setting of 3 rows and 3 columns creates a standard 9-piece sliding puzzle, while higher numbers increase the difficulty.
  4. Click Close at the bottom of the adjustments window.

Step 5: How to Solve the Puzzle

Click on any tile adjacent to the empty black space to slide it into that slot, or click and drag pieces depending on your VLC version. Continue rearranging the moving video segments until the complete picture is restored. A small counter at the top left of the video window displays your remaining unscrambled pieces or current progress.

How to Turn Off the Puzzle Filter

To return to normal playback, reopen Tools > Effects and Filters > Video Effects > Geometry, uncheck the Puzzle game box, and click Close. VLC saves this setting across sessions, so you must manually disable it to prevent future videos from opening as puzzles.
