How to Stop VLC from Auto Resizing Window

By default, VLC Media Player automatically adjusts the dimensions of its player window to match the native resolution of whatever video file you open. This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough to disable this feature on both Windows and macOS, allowing you to maintain a consistent window size regardless of the media being played.

Windows and Linux

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  3. Select the Interface tab located at the top left of the Preferences window.
  4. Locate the Look and feel section.
  5. Uncheck the box labeled Resize interface to video size.
  6. Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner.
  7. Close and reopen VLC for the setting to take effect.

macOS

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on VLC media player in the Apple menu bar at the top of your screen and select Settings... (or press Cmd + ,).
  3. Ensure the Interface tab is selected.
  4. In the settings list, find and uncheck the option labeled Resize interface to native video size.
  5. Click Save to confirm your changes.
  6. Restart VLC to apply the new behavior.

Once disabled, VLC will retain the current window size and position when loading new video files, scaling the video within the existing boundaries rather than forcing the player window to expand or shrink.
