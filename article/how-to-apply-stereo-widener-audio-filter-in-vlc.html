How to Apply Stereo Widener Audio Filter in VLC

This guide explains how to enable and configure the stereo widener audio filter in VLC Media Player. Applying this filter expands the soundstage of your audio tracks, creating a richer, more immersive listening experience through headphones or external stereo speakers.

Method 1: Enable Stereo Widener for the Current Session

To apply the filter immediately to the currently playing media:

  1. Open VLC Media Player and start playing your audio or video file.
  2. Click on the Tools menu at the top of the window and select Effects and Filters (or press Ctrl + E on Windows / Cmd + E on macOS).
  3. In the Adjustments and Effects window, select the Audio Effects tab.
  4. Click on the Stereo Widener tab.
  5. Check the Enable box.
  6. Adjust the sliders to customize your sound:
    • Delay time: Changes the delay between stereo channels to expand perceived width.
    • Feedback gain: Adjusts the amount of delayed signal fed back into the soundstage.
    • Crossfeed: Controls how much signal crosses from one channel to the other.
    • Dry mix: Controls the level of the original, unprocessed audio.
    • Wet mix: Controls the level of the widened, processed audio.
  7. Click Close when satisfied. The changes take effect immediately.

Method 2: Enable Stereo Widener Permanently

To ensure the stereo widener filter remains active every time you launch VLC:

  1. Click on Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  2. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, locate Show settings and select the All radio button to switch to Advanced Preferences.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Audio > Filters.
  4. In the main panel under Audio filters, check the box labeled Stereo widener audio filter.
  5. To adjust the default values, expand the Filters menu in the left sidebar and click on Stereo widener.
  6. Set your preferred default levels for delay time, feedback gain, crossfeed, and mix levels.
  7. Click Save at the bottom right.
  8. Restart VLC Media Player for the permanent changes to take effect.
