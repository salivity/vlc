How to Adjust Video Aspect Ratio in VLC

You can manually adjust the video aspect ratio during playback in VLC Media Player to fix stretched, squished, or incorrectly formatted video files. VLC allows you to instantly switch between preset aspect ratios, cycle through them using a hotkey, or set a custom default ratio directly from the player interface.

The Fastest Method: Use the Hotkey

The quickest way to adjust the aspect ratio while a video is playing is to use the keyboard shortcut:

  1. Click on the active VLC playback window.
  2. Press the A key on your keyboard.
  3. Keep pressing A to cycle through the available presets: Default, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 16:10, 2.21:1, and 2.35:1.
  4. Stop pressing when the video displays at your preferred proportions.

Adjust via the Menu Bar

If you prefer using your mouse, you can select an exact aspect ratio from the main menu:

  1. While the video is playing, click Video on the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Aspect Ratio.
  3. Select your desired ratio from the list (such as 16:9 for standard widescreen or 4:3 for older standard-definition content).

Adjust via the Right-Click Context Menu

You can also adjust the ratio without moving to the top bar:

  1. Right-click anywhere on the playing video.
  2. Navigate to Video > Aspect Ratio.
  3. Choose the ratio that fits your display.

How to Set a Custom Aspect Ratio

If the preset options do not fix your video, you can define a custom ratio through the advanced preferences:

  1. Go to Tools on the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on macOS).
  2. At the bottom-left corner of the preferences window, select All under Show settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click on Video.
  4. In the right panel, find the Source aspect ratio field.
  5. Enter your desired ratio manually (for example, 16:9, 4:3, or a custom value like 21:9).
  6. Click Save and restart VLC for the setting to take effect.

Aspect Ratio vs. Crop

If changing the aspect ratio stretches the image unnaturally, you may need to crop the video instead:

  • Aspect Ratio (Hotkey: A): Stretches or squeezes the existing frame into a new proportion without removing content.
  • Crop (Hotkey: C): Trims the edges (top/bottom or sides) to fit a specific shape without distorting the image geometry.
