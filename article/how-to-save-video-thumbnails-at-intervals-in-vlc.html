How to Save Video Thumbnails at Intervals in VLC

VLC Media Player includes a built-in feature known as the Scene video filter, which automatically extracts and saves frames from a video at designated intervals. This guide explains how to enable advanced settings in VLC, configure output parameters such as image format and capture frequency, and properly disable the filter once frame extraction is complete.

Step 1: Access Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Navigate to Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, locate the Show settings section and select the All radio button to unlock advanced configuration options.

Step 2: Configure the Scene Filter Settings

  1. In the left-hand sidebar, scroll down to the Video section.
  2. Expand Filters and click on Scene filter.
  3. Configure the extraction settings:
    • Image format: Choose your preferred format (e.g., png or jpeg).
    • Image width and height: Enter specific pixel dimensions, or leave them set to -1 to retain the native resolution of the video.
    • Filename prefix: Enter a prefix for your output files (e.g., thumbnail_ or scene_).
    • Directory path prefix: Enter the absolute path to the folder where VLC should save the extracted images (e.g., C:\Thumbnails on Windows or /Users/username/Thumbnails on macOS). Ensure this folder already exists.
    • Recording ratio: This setting determines how frequently frames are saved. The ratio represents the number of frames between captures. To calculate the ratio for a time interval, multiply the video's frame rate (FPS) by your desired interval in seconds. For example, if a video runs at 30 FPS and you want a thumbnail every 5 seconds, set the recording ratio to 150 (30 × 5).

Step 3: Activate the Scene Filter

  1. In the left-hand sidebar, click directly on the main Filters category (located under Video).
  2. Look through the list of video filter modules on the right.
  3. Check the box next to Scene video filter.
  4. Click Save to apply your changes.

Step 4: Extract the Thumbnails

  1. Open and play the video file you wish to process.
  2. Let the video play normally. VLC will extract and save images in the background into your selected output directory.
  3. Stop or pause the video when frame capture is complete.

Step 5: Disable the Scene Filter

The Scene filter remains active across all playback until explicitly turned off. Leaving it enabled will cause VLC to extract frames from every video you open.

  1. Go back to Tools > Preferences.
  2. Select All under Show settings.
  3. Click on Filters under the Video category.
  4. Uncheck Scene video filter.
  5. Click Save.
