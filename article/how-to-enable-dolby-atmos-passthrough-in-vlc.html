How to Enable Dolby Atmos Passthrough in VLC

To experience uncompressed Dolby Atmos audio through an external AV receiver or soundbar, VLC Media Player must be configured to pass the raw audio stream directly to your audio hardware without decoding it first. This guide explains how to enable audio passthrough in VLC settings, configure the appropriate output module, and set up your operating system so your external receiver can recognize and decode the Dolby Atmos metadata.

Prerequisites

  • An external AV receiver or soundbar that supports Dolby Atmos.
  • An HDMI connection (HDMI ARC/eARC or direct HDMI input) connecting your computer to the receiver, as optical cables (SPDIF) do not support the bandwidth required for Dolby Atmos (TrueHD or Dolby Digital Plus).
  • Media files containing a Dolby Atmos audio track (encoded in either TrueHD or E-AC-3).

Step 1: Enable Passthrough in VLC Preferences

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. Click the Audio tab at the top of the Preferences window.
  4. Under the Output section, locate the HDMI/SPDIF audio passthrough dropdown menu.
  5. Change the setting from Disabled to Enabled.

Step 2: Configure the Audio Output Module

To ensure the signal passes through bit-perfect without interference from the system mixer, configure VLC's advanced audio output settings:

  1. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, under Show settings, select the All radio button.
  2. In the left-hand navigation pane, expand Audio and click on Output modules.
  3. In the right-hand pane, find the Audio output module dropdown menu.
  4. Change it from Automatic to DirectX audio output or Windows Multimedia Device output (WASAPI).
  5. In the left pane, click directly on the module you selected (e.g., DirectX or WaveOut) to ensure the correct output device (your HDMI or AV receiver) is selected.
  6. Click Save to apply the changes.
  7. Completely close and restart VLC Media Player.

Step 3: Configure Operating System Sound Settings

For passthrough to work properly, your operating system must allow VLC exclusive control over your HDMI audio device.

  1. Open the Windows Control Panel and navigate to Sound.
  2. Under the Playback tab, right-click your connected AV receiver or HDMI display device and select Properties.
  3. Go to the Advanced tab.
  4. Under the Exclusive Mode section, check both:
    • Allow applications to take exclusive control of this device
    • Give exclusive mode applications priority
  5. Go to the Supported Formats tab and verify that Dolby TrueHD and Dolby Digital Plus are listed and checked.
  6. Click Apply, then OK.

Verification

Play your Dolby Atmos file in VLC. Once playback begins, check the front display panel or on-screen display of your external receiver. It should display Atmos, Dolby TrueHD, or Dolby Digital Plus, confirming that raw bitstream passthrough is active and that the receiver is handling the decoding.
