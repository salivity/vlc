How to Control VLC on PC Using a Phone App

You can easily control a desktop instance of VLC media player using a mobile phone app by utilizing VLC's built-in HTTP web interface. By enabling this feature on your computer and connecting your mobile device to the same local Wi-Fi network, third-party remote apps on Android and iOS can control playback, adjust volume, manage playlists, and browse media files directly from your smartphone.

Prerequisites

  • A desktop computer running VLC Media Player (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
  • A smartphone (Android or iOS) connected to the same Wi-Fi network as the computer.
  • A VLC remote app installed on your phone (such as "VLC Mobile Remote" or "VLC Remote").

Step 1: Enable the Web Interface in Desktop VLC

  1. Open VLC on your computer.
  2. Go to Tools > Preferences (or VLC > Preferences on macOS).
  3. At the bottom-left of the preferences window, under Show settings, select the All radio button.
  4. In the left sidebar, navigate to Interface > Main interfaces.
  5. Check the box next to Web.
  6. In the left sidebar, expand Main interfaces and click on Lua.
  7. Under Lua HTTP, enter a password of your choice in the Password field. Leave the username blank.
  8. Click Save and restart VLC so the changes take effect.

Step 2: Allow VLC Through the Firewall

If prompted by your operating system, allow VLC to communicate across private networks.

  • Windows: Go to Windows Defender Firewall settings, select "Allow an app through firewall," locate VLC media player, and ensure the "Private" network box is checked.
  • macOS: Go to System Settings > Network > Firewall, and ensure VLC is set to allow incoming connections.

Step 3: Find Your Computer's Local IP Address

Your mobile app will need your computer's local IP address to establish a connection.

  • Windows: Open the Command Prompt, type ipconfig, press Enter, and look for the IPv4 Address (typically in the format 192.168.x.x).
  • macOS: Open System Settings > Wi-Fi (or Network), click Details next to your connected network, and locate the IP Address.

Step 4: Configure the Mobile App

  1. Launch your chosen VLC remote app on your phone.
  2. Most apps feature an automatic setup wizard that scans the local network for running VLC instances.
  3. If the app does not find your computer automatically, select Add Manually.
  4. Enter your computer's local IP address, set the port to 8080 (the default VLC port), and enter the Lua password you configured in Step 1.
  5. Save the connection.

Once connected, you can use your phone as a full-featured remote control to pause, play, seek, adjust audio, and select subtitles on your desktop VLC player.
