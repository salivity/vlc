Is VLC Available for iOS Devices?

Yes, VLC media player is fully available for iOS devices, including the iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch. This article provides a quick overview of its availability on the Apple App Store, details its key features for mobile users, and explains how it handles various file formats without requiring conversion. Whether you want to stream content from a local server or sync videos via cloud storage, the iOS version of this popular open-source media player offers a robust solution for mobile entertainment.

How to Get VLC on iOS

VLC for iOS is completely free and can be downloaded directly from the official Apple App Store. It requires no hidden subscriptions or in-app purchases. The app is optimized for both iPhone and iPad screens, ensuring a seamless user experience across different device sizes.

Key Features and Capabilities

Unlike the native iOS video player, VLC brings a wealth of desktop-class features to your mobile device:

  • Broad Format Support: It plays almost all video and audio formats, including MKV, MP4, AVI, WMV, and FLV, without needing pre-conversion.
  • Advanced Subtitles: It fully supports advanced subtitles, including SSA compatibility, multi-track audio, and playback speed control.
  • File Synchronization: Users can sync files directly through Wi-Fi upload, iTunes, or popular cloud services like Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive.
  • Network Streaming: It supports network streams, including HLS, MMS, or RTSP, alongside media sharing from SMB, FTP, and Plex servers.
