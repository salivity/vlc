How to Clear Recent Playback History in VLC

VLC Media Player automatically keeps a record of your recently played media files to help you quickly resume playback. While this feature is convenient, you may want to remove this list to maintain your privacy or keep your media player organized. This guide provides direct, step-by-step instructions on how to immediately clear your recent media playback history and how to permanently prevent VLC from saving your playback history in the future.

How to Clear Recent Media History Immediately

To remove the list of recently played files in VLC:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Media in the top menu bar (on macOS, click File).
  3. Hover your cursor over Open Recent Media (on macOS, hover over Open Recent).
  4. Click Clear at the bottom of the list.

Your recent playback list within the VLC application will now be empty.

How to Permanently Disable Playback History

If you do not want VLC to track or store your media history going forward, you can turn the feature off entirely.

On Windows:

  1. Open VLC and navigate to Tools > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  2. Ensure the Interface tab is selected at the top.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy / Network Interaction section.
  4. Uncheck the box labeled Save recently played items.
  5. Click Save to apply the changes.

On macOS:

  1. Open VLC and click VLC in the top menu bar, then select Settings... (or press Cmd + ,).
  2. Select the Interface tab.
  3. Locate the privacy settings and uncheck Keep recent items.
  4. Click Save.

Clearing VLC History from the Windows Taskbar

Even after clearing history inside VLC, Windows may still show recently played media in the VLC taskbar Jump List. To remove these:

  1. Right-click the VLC icon pinned to your Windows taskbar.
  2. Right-click the specific file name under the "Recent" section.
  3. Select Remove from this list.

Alternatively, disabling history within VLC's preferences (as outlined above) will automatically clear and stop new entries from populating the Windows taskbar Jump List.
