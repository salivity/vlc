How to Lock the Volume Slider in VLC

Accidental volume adjustments in VLC Media Player frequently happen when scrolling through a video or misclicking the on-screen interface. While VLC does not offer a single button labeled "Lock Volume," you can permanently fix this issue by removing the volume slider widget from the control bar and unbinding hotkeys and mouse wheel scrolling. This guide covers the exact steps required to remove the slider and eliminate all accidental volume triggers so your playback level stays constant.

Method 1: Remove the Volume Slider from the Toolbar

The most foolproof way to prevent clicking or dragging the volume slider is to remove it entirely from the main interface controls.

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Go to the top menu and select Tools > Customize Interface.
  3. Under the Toolbars Elements section, locate the visual layout of your current controls (typically found under Line 2).
  4. Find the Volume or Volume Slider widget in the layout box.
  5. Click and drag the volume element out of the layout and drop it into the gray area containing available elements, or drag it into the Trash area.
  6. Click Close.

The volume slider will disappear from the VLC toolbar, preventing any accidental mouse interaction. You can restore it at any time by revisiting the same menu.

Method 2: Disable Mouse Wheel Volume Adjustments

By default, rolling your mouse wheel over the playback window or the controls raises or lowers the volume. Disabling this stops the most common source of accidental changes.

  1. Click on Tools > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows).
  2. At the bottom-left corner of the window, select All under Show settings to access advanced configuration.
  3. In the left sidebar, expand Interface > Main interfaces, then select Qt.
  4. Scroll down the right panel until you find the Mouse wheel action dropdown.
  5. Change the selection from controlling volume to Position control or choose to ignore the wheel.
  6. Click Save and restart VLC for the change to take effect.

Method 3: Remove Volume Keyboard Shortcuts

To prevent accidental keystrokes from altering your volume, clear the default volume hotkeys.

  1. Open Tools > Preferences.
  2. Switch back to the Simple settings view in the bottom-left corner if needed.
  3. Click on the Hotkeys tab at the top.
  4. Scroll through the list and locate:
    • Volume up
    • Volume down
    • Mute
  5. Double-click each hotkey entry and click Unset (or press Clear).
  6. Click Save.

Combining these adjustments completely secures your audio level, ensuring VLC maintains a static volume that cannot be moved unintentionally during playback.
