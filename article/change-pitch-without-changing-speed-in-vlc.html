Change Pitch Without Changing Speed in VLC

Adjusting the pitch of an audio or video track in VLC Media Player without altering the playback tempo is entirely possible using the software's built-in audio filters. This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to use VLC's native Pitch Shifter tool to raise or lower audio keys in real time while keeping playback speed completely normal.

Adjust Pitch Using Audio Effects (Real-Time)

The quickest way to shift pitch during playback is through the Adjustments and Effects window.

  1. Open VLC Media Player and start playing your media file.
  2. In the top menu bar, click on Tools and select Effects and Filters (or press Ctrl + E on Windows / Cmd + E on macOS).
  3. In the window that appears, click on the Audio Effects tab.
  4. Click on the Advanced sub-tab.
  5. Check the box labeled Pitch Shifter.
  6. Use the slider or numeric input to raise or lower the pitch:
    • Moving the value above zero increases the pitch (higher key).
    • Moving the value below zero lowers the pitch (deeper key).
    • Each integer corresponds to a semitone shift.
  7. Click Close. The pitch modification takes effect immediately while the video or audio continues playing at standard speed (1.00x).

Make the Pitch Setting Persistent Across Sessions

By default, VLC resets audio filter settings when the application closes. If you want the pitch modification to apply automatically every time VLC launches, configure it in the main preferences:

  1. Click on Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  2. In the bottom-left corner under Show settings, select the All radio button to access advanced options.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Audio > Filters.
  4. In the list of available audio filters, locate and check Pitch shifter.
  5. In the left panel, expand the Filters menu under Audio and click directly on Pitch shifter.
  6. Enter your preferred semitone shift in the configuration field.
  7. Click Save and restart VLC Media Player for the settings to take effect permanently.
