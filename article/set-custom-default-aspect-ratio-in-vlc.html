Set Custom Default Aspect Ratio in VLC

VLC Media Player plays videos using their native aspect ratios by default, but you can override this behavior to force a specific ratio across all playback sessions. By accessing VLC’s advanced settings, you can define a custom default aspect ratio—such as 16:9, 4:3, or 21:9—ensuring that every video automatically opens with your preferred display dimensions without needing manual adjustment each time.

Steps to Set a Default Aspect Ratio in VLC

  1. Open Preferences
    Launch VLC Media Player. In the top menu, click on Tools and select Preferences, or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux (Cmd + , on macOS).

  2. Show All Settings
    By default, VLC displays the simple settings menu. Look at the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window under Show settings and select the All radio button to access the advanced options.

  3. Navigate to Video Settings
    In the left sidebar of the Advanced Preferences window, locate and click directly on the Video category (do not expand the subcategories; click the main "Video" heading).

  4. Enter Your Custom Aspect Ratio
    Scroll down the right pane until you find the Source aspect ratio input field. Type your desired aspect ratio exactly into the box. Common options include:

    • 16:9 (Standard widescreen)
    • 4:3 (Standard full-screen/retro format)
    • 21:9 (Ultrawide format)
    • 2.35:1 or 2.39:1 (Cinematic widescreen)

  5. Save and Restart
    Click the Save button in the lower-right corner to apply the changes. Close VLC Media Player completely and reopen it.

Every video played after the restart will automatically conform to the defined aspect ratio. To revert back to the default behavior in the future, return to the Source aspect ratio field, delete the text so the field is blank, and click Save.
