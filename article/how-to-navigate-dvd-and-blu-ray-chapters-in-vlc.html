How to Navigate DVD and Blu-ray Chapters in VLC

Navigating through chapter markers on a DVD or Blu-ray in VLC Media Player allows you to jump directly to specific scenes without manually scrubbing through the playback timeline. This guide outlines the primary methods for chapter navigation in VLC across desktop systems, including the standard playback controls, the main navigation menu, right-click context options, and keyboard shortcuts.

Method 1: Using the On-Screen Playback Buttons

The simplest way to jump between chapters is using VLC's built-in interface controls:

  1. Insert your disc and start playback in VLC.
  2. Locate the playback control bar at the bottom of the player window.
  3. Click the Next Chapter button (represented by an arrow pointing right toward a vertical bar) to skip forward to the next chapter marker.
  4. Click the Previous Chapter button (represented by an arrow pointing left toward a vertical bar) to restart the current chapter or return to the preceding one.

Note: During disc playback, these buttons automatically switch function from playlist navigation to chapter navigation.

Method 2: Selecting Specific Chapters from the Menu Bar

To jump to a specific chapter by number rather than cycling through sequentially:

  1. Click on the Playback menu at the top of the VLC application window.
  2. Hover over Chapter.
  3. A list of available chapters for the current title will appear. Click on the desired chapter number to jump immediately to that point in the video.

Method 3: Using the Right-Click Context Menu

You can access chapter markers without moving your mouse to the top menu bar:

  1. Right-click anywhere inside the active video display area.
  2. Hover your cursor over the Playback option.
  3. Hover over Chapter and select your desired chapter from the drop-down list.

Method 4: Navigating via Disc Root Menus

If you are playing a standard DVD or a Blu-ray with active menu support:

  1. Access the main disc menu by clicking Playback in the top menu and selecting Title > Disc Menu, or by clicking the DVD Menu button on the bottom control bar (if visible).
  2. Use your mouse to click directly on the "Scene Selection" or "Chapter Selection" options provided by the disc's authoring menu.
  3. If mouse clicks are unresponsive on Blu-ray menus, use your keyboard's arrow keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) to highlight options, and press Enter to confirm.

Method 5: Using Default Keyboard Shortcuts

VLC offers default hotkeys to skip chapters quickly without the mouse:

  • Next Chapter: Press Shift + Cmd + Right Arrow (macOS) or use the dedicated media key for "Next Track" on your keyboard (Windows/Linux).
  • Previous Chapter: Press Shift + Cmd + Left Arrow (macOS) or the dedicated media key for "Previous Track" (Windows/Linux).

To assign custom single-key hotkeys for skipping chapters on Windows or Linux:

  1. Go to Tools > Preferences.
  2. Click on the Hotkeys tab at the top or bottom left.
  3. Locate the actions labeled Next chapter and Previous chapter.
  4. Double-click the hotkey column for either action, press your preferred key combination, and click Save.
