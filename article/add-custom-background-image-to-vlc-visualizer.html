Add Custom Background Image to VLC Visualizer

This guide explains how to replace the default blank screen or VLC cone icon with a custom background image during audio playback. Whether you want to attach dedicated cover art to individual tracks or display a persistent background image while audio visualizers are active, VLC Media Player provides built-in tools to customize your audio listening experience.

Method 1: Assign Custom Cover Art via Media Information

The simplest way to display a custom image during audio playback is by assigning cover art directly within VLC. This image serves as the main backdrop whenever the track plays.

  1. Open VLC Media Player and play the audio file.
  2. Click Tools in the top menu bar and select Media Information (or press Ctrl + I on Windows / Cmd + I on macOS).
  3. In the lower-right corner of the General tab, locate the placeholder area (typically the VLC cone logo).
  4. Right-click the placeholder image and choose Add cover art from file.
  5. Browse your computer, select the desired image file (JPG or PNG), and click Open.
  6. Click Save Metadata at the bottom of the window, then close the dialog.

The custom image will now appear as the visual backdrop whenever that file plays.

Method 2: Overlay an Image Over Active Visualizers

If you want an image to display permanently over an active audio visualization (such as Goom or ProjectM), use VLC’s video logo filter.

  1. Open VLC and navigate to Tools > Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  2. In the bottom-left corner under Show settings, switch from Simple to All.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Video > Subpictures filters.
  4. In the list of filters on the right pane, check the box next to Logo video filter.
  5. Expand the Subpictures filters tree in the left pane and click Logo overlay.
  6. In the Logo file name field, enter the full file path to your background image (e.g., C:\Images\background.png).
  7. Adjust the Logo X coordinate, Logo Y coordinate, and Logo opacity values as needed to position the image across the screen.
  8. Click Save and restart VLC for the changes to take effect.

Method 3: Replace Global Album Art in VLC App Data

To set a standard image across tracks without embedded artwork, replace the cached art in VLC's system directory.

  1. Close VLC completely.
  2. Open your system's file manager and navigate to the VLC art cache folder:
    • Windows: %appdata%\vlc\art\
    • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/org.videolan.vlc/art/
    • Linux: ~/.cache/vlc/art/
  3. Locate the folder corresponding to your artist or album.
  4. Replace the existing art.jpg or art.png file with your custom image, ensuring you rename your new file to match the original filename exactly.
  5. Reopen VLC and start playback.
