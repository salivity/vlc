Can VLC Stream to Smart TV via Miracast?

VLC Media Player cannot directly stream video files to a smart TV using Miracast on its own, because Miracast functions as an operating system-level screen mirroring protocol rather than an in-app network streaming feature. However, you can easily achieve the same result by using your computer or smartphone's native Miracast display settings while playing your local video file in VLC. This guide explains why native Miracast streaming is absent in VLC, how to project your screen to your TV using system-level Miracast, and what native streaming alternatives VLC provides.

Why VLC Lacks Direct Miracast Streaming

Miracast is often described as "HDMI over Wi-Fi." It relies on Wi-Fi Direct and functions by encoding your device's entire display output (or an extended desktop) into an H.264 video stream at the hardware and operating system level.

Because Miracast operates through your device's graphics drivers and network adapters, individual desktop applications like VLC cannot connect directly to a Miracast receiver independently of the operating system. Instead, VLC simply renders video to your screen, and the operating system is responsible for transmitting those frames over Miracast.

How to Watch VLC on a Smart TV Using Miracast

To watch a local video played via VLC on a Miracast-compatible smart TV, you must initiate the Miracast connection through your host operating system.

On Windows 10 and 11

  1. Turn on your smart TV and ensure Miracast (often labeled as Screen Mirroring, Wireless Display, or AllShare Cast) is enabled.
  2. Ensure both your PC and your TV are connected to the same local network, or that Wi-Fi is enabled on your PC.
  3. Press the keyboard shortcut Windows Key + K to open the Cast/Project menu.
  4. Select your smart TV from the list of available wireless displays.
  5. Once connected, open VLC Media Player on your PC.
  6. Open your local video file and enter fullscreen mode (F11 or double-click the video). The audio and video will play directly on your TV.

On Android

  1. Swipe down from the top of the screen to open the Quick Settings panel.
  2. Tap Cast, Smart View, or Screen Mirroring (naming varies by manufacturer).
  3. Select your smart TV from the list.
  4. Open the VLC app on Android, select your local video file, and play it.

Alternative: VLC’s Native "Renderer" Feature

If you want to stream video files directly to your TV without mirroring your entire screen, VLC includes built-in streaming functionality for Google Cast (Chromecast) and UPnP/DLNA devices.

  1. Open VLC on your computer.
  2. Go to the top menu bar and click Playback.
  3. Hover over Renderer.
  4. VLC will scan your local network for compatible devices.
  5. Select your smart TV or streaming device from the list.
  6. Open your local file and click Play.

This method sends only the raw media stream to the TV, allowing you to continue using your computer display for other tasks while the video plays on the television.
