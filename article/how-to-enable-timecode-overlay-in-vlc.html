How to Enable Timecode Overlay in VLC

This guide explains how to display a permanent on-screen timecode overlay during video playback in VLC media player. By utilizing VLC’s built-in Marquee subpicture filter, you can render real-time playback elapsed time, remaining time, or total duration directly onto the video screen without modifying the underlying media file.

Step 1: Access Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC media player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. In the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, under Show settings, select the All radio button to access advanced options.

Step 2: Enable the Marquee Display Filter

  1. In the left-hand sidebar, navigate to Video > Subtitles / OSD.
  2. Scroll down the right pane to the Subpicture filter section.
  3. Check the box labeled Marquee display.

Step 3: Configure the Timecode Settings

  1. In the left-hand sidebar, expand Subtitles / OSD by clicking the arrow next to it, then select Marquee.
  2. In the Text field, enter the format code for the timecode information you want to display:
    • $T displays the current playback time (elapsed time).
    • $D displays the total duration of the file.
    • $T / $D displays the current elapsed time alongside the total duration (e.g., 00:04:12 / 01:30:00).
    • $L displays the remaining time left in the video.
  3. Use the Position dropdown menu to select where the timecode appears on screen (such as Top-Right, Bottom-Right, or Bottom-Left).
  4. Adjust optional parameters such as Text size in pixels, Color, and Opacity to fit your visibility preferences.

Step 4: Save and Apply

  1. Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner to apply the changes.
  2. Close VLC and restart the player for the filter changes to take full effect.

When you open any video file, the timecode overlay will now automatically appear in your designated location on the playback screen. To disable the overlay later, return to Video > Subtitles / OSD in the Advanced Preferences and uncheck the Marquee display option.
