How to Bookmark Timestamps in VLC Media Player

VLC Media Player includes a built-in bookmarking feature that lets you mark and instantly return to specific scenes or timestamps in any video. While the standard bookmarks are temporary and automatically clear once the video player is closed, you can easily create, manage, and even permanently save your custom timestamps by saving your session as a playlist file.

How to Create a Bookmark in VLC

  1. Open your video file in VLC Media Player.
  2. Play the video or scrub the timeline to the exact moment you want to mark.
  3. Access the bookmark manager:
    • Windows/Linux: Press Ctrl + B on your keyboard, or click Playback in the top menu, hover over Custom Bookmarks, and select Manage.
    • macOS: Press Cmd + B, or click Window in the menu bar and select Bookmarks.
  4. In the "Edit Bookmarks" window that appears, click the Create button. A new entry showing the exact time and bytes will appear in the list.

Navigating and Managing Bookmarks

  • Jump to a timestamp: Open the bookmark list (Ctrl + B or Cmd + B) and double-click the bookmark you want to view. You can also quickly access them via the Playback > Custom Bookmarks menu without opening the management window.
  • Delete a bookmark: Open the Edit Bookmarks window, select the entry you want to remove, and click Delete (or Clear to remove all).

How to Save Bookmarks Permanently

By default, VLC deletes all created bookmarks as soon as you close the application. To preserve your saved timestamps for future viewing sessions, save the playback session as a playlist:

  1. Create all your desired bookmarks for the video.
  2. Click Media in the top menu and select Save Playlist to File... (or press Ctrl + Y).
  3. Set the file format to XSPF playlist (*.xspf) and choose your destination folder.
  4. Whenever you want to watch the video with your bookmarks intact, open this .xspf playlist file instead of the original video file. Your bookmarks will be restored and accessible via Ctrl + B.
