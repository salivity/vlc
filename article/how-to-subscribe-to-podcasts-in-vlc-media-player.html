How to Subscribe to Podcasts in VLC Media Player

VLC Media Player includes a built-in podcast manager that allows users to subscribe to, stream, and download audio podcasts directly via RSS feeds. While widely recognized as a versatile media player, VLC eliminates the need for dedicated podcast software by handling subscriptions natively inside its playlist interface. This guide explains how to access the podcast feature, add new feeds, and control episode playback directly on your computer.

How to Subscribe to an Audio Podcast

VLC does not have a built-in directory, so you must have the direct RSS feed URL of the podcast you wish to add.

  1. Open the Playlist View: Launch VLC Media Player. On Windows or Linux, navigate to View > Playlist, or press Ctrl + L. On macOS, select Window > Playlist, or press Cmd + 1.
  2. Locate the Podcasts Section: Look at the left sidebar under the Internet category. You will see an entry labeled Podcasts.
  3. Add the Feed: Hover over the Podcasts entry and click the + (plus) icon that appears next to it. Alternatively, right-click Podcasts and select Subscribe.
  4. Enter the URL: Paste the RSS feed URL of the podcast into the dialog box and click OK.
  5. Load the Episodes: VLC will connect to the server and fetch the available episodes. The podcast title will appear as a sub-item under the Podcasts section.

Managing and Playing Episodes

Once the feed is loaded, you can interact with episodes just like standard media files:

  • Streaming: Double-click any episode in the playlist to begin streaming it immediately over the internet.
  • Downloading: If you want to listen offline, right-click an episode, select Save..., and choose a destination folder on your local drive.
  • Refreshing Feeds: To check for newly released episodes, right-click the specific podcast title in the sidebar and select Reload.
  • Unsubscribing: When you no longer wish to follow a show, right-click the podcast name in the sidebar and select Unsubscribe or press the - (minus) button.

Capabilities and Limitations

Managing podcasts in VLC is functional and lightweight, but it differs from dedicated podcatchers:

  • Pros: Free, lightweight, cross-platform, contains no ads, supports standard playback controls (speed adjustment, equalizers), and requires no third-party account.
  • Cons: Lacks an integrated search directory (manual RSS entry is mandatory), does not support cross-device synchronization, and offers limited automatic episode downloading or progress tracking between sessions.
