Can VLC Play Lossless FLAC Files?

Yes, VLC Media Player fully supports playing lossless FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec) audio files out of the box. As one of the most versatile media players available, VLC includes native codecs for FLAC, meaning you do not need to download any additional plugins or software to enjoy high-fidelity audio. This article covers VLC's compatibility with FLAC across different devices, how to play these files, and tips for optimizing your audio settings for the best listening experience.

FLAC Support Across Different Platforms

VLC Media Player handles FLAC files seamlessly across almost every major operating system. Whether you are on a desktop or a mobile device, the playback experience remains consistent.

  • Windows and macOS: The desktop versions of VLC offer robust support for FLAC, easily handling high-resolution audio files (such as 24-bit/192kHz tracks) without stuttering.
  • Linux: Because FLAC is an open-source format, Linux distributions running VLC handle it natively and efficiently.
  • Android and iOS: The mobile versions of VLC also support FLAC, allowing you to listen to lossless audio on the go, provided your device's hardware or connected headphones can output the high-quality sound.

How to Play FLAC Files in VLC

Playing a FLAC file in VLC is identical to playing any other standard audio or video file. You can use any of the following methods:

  1. Drag and Drop: Open VLC, select your FLAC file from your file manager, and drag it directly into the VLC window.
  2. Double-Click: If VLC is set as your default media player, simply double-clicking the FLAC file will launch it immediately.
  3. Media Menu: Open VLC, click on Media (or File on Mac) in the top menu, select Open File, and browse to your FLAC track.

Tips for the Best Lossless Audio Quality in VLC

While VLC plays FLAC files automatically, you can adjust a few settings to ensure you are getting the absolute best audio output from your lossless files.

Enable Audio Passthrough

If you are connecting your computer to an external Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) or a high-end audio receiver, you should allow VLC to pass the audio signal directly to the device without modifying it. Navigate to Tools > Preferences > Audio and ensure your output module is set to utilize your specific audio hardware correctly.

Adjust the Audio Effects

VLC includes a built-in 10-band equalizer. If you want to tweak the sound profile to match your headphones or speakers, go to Tools > Effects and Filters > Audio Effects. From here, you can enable the equalizer and choose a preset or manually adjust the frequency bands to enhance your lossless listening experience.
