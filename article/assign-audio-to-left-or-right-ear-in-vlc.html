Assign Audio to Left or Right Ear in VLC

VLC Media Player allows users to isolate and route audio playback to a single headphone or speaker by adjusting channel settings or applying internal audio filters. This guide explains how to quickly assign an audio channel to exclusively the left or right ear using VLC's Stereo Mode, as well as how to use the advanced Pan Filter to force any active audio track to output entirely through one side.

Method 1: Using Stereo Mode (Quick Channel Selection)

This method works best if your media file contains a dual-channel track (such as two different languages recorded onto the left and right channels) and you want to isolate one side.

  1. Open your media file in VLC.
  2. Click on Audio in the top menu bar.
  3. Hover over Stereo Mode.
  4. Select Left to route the left channel to your left ear, or select Right to route the right channel to your right ear.

(Note: Depending on your audio output settings, this may duplicate the selected channel across both ears. To enforce absolute silence in the opposite ear, use Method 2.)

Method 2: Using the Advanced Audio Pan Filter

To manually force a track to output exclusively to the left or right ear while keeping the opposite side silent, configure VLC's Pan audio filter.

  1. Click on Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on macOS).
  2. At the bottom-left corner under Show settings, select the All radio button to access advanced preferences.
  3. In the left-hand sidebar, navigate to Audio > Filters.
  4. In the list of available filters on the right, check the box for Pan audio filter.
  5. Expand the Filters tree in the left sidebar by clicking the arrow next to it, then click on Pan.
  6. Set the Panning position value:
    • Enter -1.000000 for exclusively the Left ear.
    • Enter 1.000000 for exclusively the Right ear.
    • Enter 0.000000 to return to standard centered stereo.
  7. Click Save.
  8. Restart VLC for the filter changes to take full effect.

Method 3: Selecting Specific Audio Tracks

If your file has multiple distinct audio tracks rather than split stereo channels:

  1. Click on Audio in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Audio Track.
  3. Select the desired track from the list.
  4. Apply Method 2 to shift that selected track entirely to your preferred ear.
