How to Disable OSD in VLC Completely

This guide explains how to completely turn off the On-Screen Display (OSD) messages in VLC Media Player. Disabling this feature stops VLC from showing overlay text on the video, such as volume adjustments, playback status icons, and track titles, ensuring an uninterrupted and clean viewing experience.

Steps to Disable OSD in VLC

  1. Open Preferences Launch VLC Media Player. On Windows or Linux, click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences, or press Ctrl + P. On macOS, click VLC in the top menu bar and choose Preferences, or press Cmd + ,.

  2. Navigate to Subtitles / OSD In the Preferences window, select the Subtitles / OSD tab located at the top (or left sidebar depending on the interface).

  3. Turn Off OSD Notifications Locate the section labeled On Screen Display. Uncheck the box labeled Enable On Screen Display (OSD).

  4. Disable Media Title Overlays To prevent the filename or track title from popping up when a video starts, locate the Show media title on video start option within the same settings menu and uncheck it.

  5. Save and Restart Click the Save button at the bottom right of the Preferences window to apply your changes. Close and restart VLC Media Player to ensure the new settings take full effect.
