How to Import an OPML Podcast List into VLC

VLC Media Player can stream and manage podcast feeds alongside your local media library, allowing you to centralize your listening experience. While VLC does not feature a dedicated "Import OPML" button in its podcast manager, you can easily load an entire OPML podcast subscription file into VLC using its playlist engine. This guide provides the direct, step-by-step process to open your OPML file, parse your feeds, and listen to episodes within VLC.

Step 1: Prepare Your OPML File

Ensure you have exported the OPML file from your current podcast app or RSS reader and saved it to a convenient location on your computer (such as your Desktop or Downloads folder).

Step 2: Open VLC and Access the Playlist

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Open the Playlist view by pressing Ctrl + L on Windows/Linux or Cmd + Shift + P on macOS. You can also click View in the top menu and select Playlist.

Step 3: Load the OPML File into VLC

VLC reads OPML files natively as playlist outlines:

  1. Click Media in the top menu bar (or File on macOS).
  2. Select Open File....
  3. In the file picker, navigate to your saved .opml file. If the file is not visible, change the file type dropdown from "Media Files" to "All Files (.)".
  4. Select your file and click Open.

Alternatively, you can drag and drop the .opml file directly from your file manager into the main VLC playlist window.

Step 4: Browse and Stream Your Podcasts

Once imported, the OPML structure will appear in your VLC playlist:

  1. Double-click the imported list entry to expand it.
  2. VLC will fetch the individual podcast channels listed in the file.
  3. Expand any podcast channel to reveal its available episodes, then double-click an episode to start streaming immediately.

Adding Feeds to the Dedicated Podcast Directory (Alternative)

If you prefer your podcasts to appear permanently under the Internet > Podcasts section in VLC's sidebar:

  1. Open your OPML file in any text editor (such as Notepad or TextEdit).
  2. Locate the RSS feed links inside the xmlUrl="..." tags.
  3. In VLC's playlist sidebar, hover over Podcasts under the Internet section and click the + (plus) icon.
  4. Paste the individual podcast URL and click OK.
