How to Create and Save a Playlist in VLC

VLC Media Player allows you to compile your favorite video and audio tracks into custom playlists so you can enjoy continuous playback without manual interruptions. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to build, organize, and permanently save a custom playlist in VLC on your computer.

Step 1: Open the Playlist Interface

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Click View on the top menu bar.
  3. Select Playlist from the dropdown menu, or press Ctrl + L (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + P (Mac) on your keyboard.

Step 2: Add Media Files

You can populate your playlist using either of the following methods:

  • Drag and Drop: Select audio or video files directly from your file manager and drag them into the empty space in the VLC Playlist window.
  • Menu Selection: Right-click inside the blank playlist area, choose Add File... or Add Folder..., navigate to your media location, select your files, and click Open.

Step 3: Organize Your Tracks

  • Reorder: Click and drag any track up or down to change its playback order.
  • Sort: Right-click inside the playlist, hover over Sort by, and choose your preferred parameter (such as Title, Artist, or Track Number).
  • Remove Tracks: Select an unwanted file and press the Delete key on your keyboard, or right-click the item and select Remove Selected.

Step 4: Save the Playlist File

  1. Click Media on the top menu bar.
  2. Select Save Playlist to File... (or press Ctrl + Y on Windows/Linux, Cmd + S on Mac).
  3. Choose the destination folder where you want to store your playlist file.
  4. Enter a name for your playlist in the File name field.
  5. Choose a format from the Save as type dropdown. The default XSPF format works best within VLC, while M3U playlist or M3U8 offers broader compatibility with other media players.
  6. Click Save.

How to Load Your Saved Playlist

To listen to or watch your saved playlist again, simply double-click the saved playlist file from your file browser, or open VLC, click Media > Open File..., and select the saved playlist. As long as your original media files remain in their original folders, VLC will load and play your custom queue immediately.
