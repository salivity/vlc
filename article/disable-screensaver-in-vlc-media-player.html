Disable Screensaver in VLC Media Player

This guide explains how to prevent your computer's screensaver and display sleep mode from activating while VLC Media Player is playing video or audio. By adjusting VLC’s built-in power management settings, you can ensure uninterrupted playback without altering your entire operating system's display sleep configuration.

Method 1: Using Simple Preferences

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Navigate to Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. Click on the Video tab at the top of the Preferences window.
  4. Locate the checkbox labeled Disable screensaver.
  5. Check the box to enable this feature.
  6. Click Save at the bottom right.
  7. Restart VLC Media Player to apply the changes.

Method 2: Using Advanced Preferences (Full Override)

If the standard setting fails to prevent your system screensaver from activating, VLC provides an advanced configuration option:

  1. Open VLC Media Player and go to Tools > Preferences.
  2. In the bottom-left corner under Show settings, select the All radio button to access advanced preferences.
  3. In the left-hand menu tree, click directly on Video.
  4. On the right panel, scroll down to find Disable screensaver and ensure the box is checked.
  5. Next, scroll to the Advanced section in the left sidebar and expand Power management.
  6. Ensure any power-inhibition or screensaver-blocking options present for your operating system are set to active.
  7. Click Save and restart VLC.

Once these settings are saved, VLC will block the operating system's internal screensaver and screen timeout mechanisms whenever media is actively playing.
