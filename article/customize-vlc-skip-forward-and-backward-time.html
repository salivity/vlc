Customize VLC Skip Forward and Backward Time

VLC Media Player allows users to fully customize the skip forward and skip backward time durations to match their viewing preferences. By default, VLC provides four distinct jump intervals—Very Short, Short, Medium, and Long—ranging from a few seconds to several minutes. While these settings are not immediately visible in the basic preferences menu, you can easily adjust the exact second values through the advanced interface settings on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Understanding VLC Default Jump Intervals

VLC uses four preset jump durations tied to keyboard shortcuts:

  • Very short jump: 3 seconds (Shift + Left/Right Arrow)
  • Short jump: 10 seconds (Alt + Left/Right Arrow)
  • Medium jump: 1 minute (Ctrl + Left/Right Arrow)
  • Long jump: 5 minutes (Ctrl + Alt + Left/Right Arrow)

How to Change the Skip Time Durations

To change the duration of these jumps, follow these steps:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Navigate to Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux, Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, locate the Show settings section and select the All radio button to switch to Advanced Preferences.
  4. In the left sidebar, click on Interface, then expand it and select Hotkeys settings.
  5. Scroll through the right-hand panel until you locate the Jump sizes section. Here you will find:
    • Very short jump length
    • Short jump length
    • Medium jump length
    • Long jump length
  6. Enter your desired duration in seconds for each category.
  7. Click Save at the bottom right.
  8. Restart VLC Media Player for the changes to take effect.

Once restarted, using the assigned shortcut keys will move your playback forward or backward by your newly defined time intervals.
