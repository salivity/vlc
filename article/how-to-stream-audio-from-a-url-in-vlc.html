How to Stream Audio from a URL in VLC

Streaming audio from a specific web address is a straightforward process in VLC Media Player, allowing you to listen to internet radio, podcasts, and remote audio feeds without downloading the files. This guide provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to manually enter a network URL to stream audio using VLC on both desktop computers and mobile devices, along with tips for common streaming formats.

Streaming Audio on Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux)

Follow these steps to open a direct audio link in the desktop version of VLC:

  1. Copy the Audio URL: Locate the web link for the audio stream you want to play. This must be a direct stream URL ending in extensions like .mp3, .aac, .ogg, or playlist formats like .m3u or .pls.
  2. Open VLC Media Player: Launch the VLC application on your computer.
  3. Open the Network Stream Menu:
    • Windows/Linux: Click on Media in the top menu bar, then select Open Network Stream... (or press Ctrl + N).
    • macOS: Click on File in the top menu bar, then select Open Network... (or press Cmd + N).
  4. Enter the URL: In the window that appears, make sure the Network tab is selected. Click into the text box labeled Please enter a network URL and paste your copied audio address.
  5. Start Playback: Click the Play button (or Open on macOS) at the bottom of the dialog box. VLC will buffer the connection and begin playing the audio stream.

Streaming Audio on Mobile (Android and iOS)

You can also stream direct audio links using the VLC mobile app:

  1. Copy the Audio URL from your mobile browser or source app.
  2. Open the VLC App on your Android or iOS device.
  3. Navigate to Streams:
    • Android: Tap More in the bottom navigation bar, then select Streams.
    • iOS: Tap the Network tab at the bottom of the screen, then tap Open Network Stream.
  4. Paste the URL: Tap the entry field and paste your audio stream URL.
  5. Play: Tap the arrow button or press Return to begin playback immediately.

Supported Protocols and Troubleshooting

VLC supports a wide range of network protocols for audio playback, including http://, https://, rtsp://, and mms://.

If the stream does not play:

  • Verify Direct Access: Ensure the URL points directly to an audio feed or playlist, rather than a web page with an embedded player.
  • Check Firewall Settings: Ensure your computer's firewall is not blocking VLC from accessing external network ports.
  • Inspect the Codec: Go to Tools > Codec Information (Ctrl + J or Cmd + I) while attempting playback to check if VLC recognizes the incoming data stream.
