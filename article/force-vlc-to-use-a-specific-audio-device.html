Force VLC to Use a Specific Audio Device

By default, VLC Media Player automatically routes sound through your operating system's default output. However, you can configure VLC to always output sound to a specific set of headphones, external speakers, or a dedicated audio interface without altering your system-wide audio settings. This guide covers how to permanently lock VLC to your preferred audio hardware as well as how to switch devices on the fly during media playback.

Permanently Set a Specific Audio Device

To ensure VLC always defaults to a specific audio output device regardless of your system settings, follow these steps:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Navigate to Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on macOS).
  3. Click on the Audio tab at the top of the Preferences window.
  4. Locate the Output section:
    • Click the Output module drop-down menu. Change it from Automatic to a specific module, such as DirectX audio output or WaveOut audio output on Windows (or CoreAudio on macOS).
    • Once a dedicated module is selected, the Device drop-down menu will unlock.
  5. Click the Device menu and choose the specific output hardware (e.g., your specific USB headset or external monitor speakers) you want VLC to use.
  6. Click Save at the bottom right.
  7. Close and restart VLC Media Player for the changes to take effect.

Advanced Configuration (If the Device Menu Remains Inactive)

If the device dropdown menu remains disabled or your desired device does not appear, configure it through the advanced preferences:

  1. Open Tools > Preferences.
  2. At the bottom-left corner under Show settings, select the All radio button.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Audio > Output modules.
  4. Click directly on your preferred output module (such as DirectX).
  5. In the right pane, locate the Output device option, select your preferred hardware from the list, and click Save.
  6. Restart VLC.

Temporarily Switch Audio Devices During Playback

If you only need to change the audio device for your current session without permanently altering default configurations:

  1. Begin playing your media file in VLC.
  2. Click Audio in the top menu bar.
  3. Hover over Audio Device.
  4. Select the output device you want to use from the sub-menu.

VLC will immediately reroute the audio stream to the selected hardware until the application is closed.
