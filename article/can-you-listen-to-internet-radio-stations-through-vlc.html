Can You Listen to Internet Radio Stations Through VLC?

Yes, you can absolutely use VLC Media Player to stream internet radio stations, making it a versatile alternative to dedicated streaming apps or browser tabs. This article provides a quick overview of how VLC handles online audio streams, followed by step-by-step instructions on accessing VLC's built-in station directories and manually adding custom radio URLs for uninterrupted listening.

How to Access Internet Radio in VLC

VLC Media Player comes equipped with built-in directories that allow you to browse and stream thousands of public radio stations without needing external links.

  1. Open the Playlist: Launch VLC and open the Playlist view. You can do this by pressing Ctrl + L on Windows/Linux or Cmd + Shift + P on a Mac.
  2. Navigate to the Internet Section: In the left-hand sidebar, look for the Internet category.
  3. Select a Directory: Click on options like Icecast Radio Directory or Jamendo Selections. VLC will take a moment to download and populate the list with available live streams.
  4. Play a Station: Double-click any station or genre folder in the main window to start listening immediately.

Streaming Custom Radio Station URLs

If your favorite local or global radio station isn't listed in the default directories, you can still play it by using its direct streaming URL.

  1. Find the Stream URL: Go to the website of the radio station you want to hear and look for their direct streaming link (often ending in formats like .pls, .m3u, .mp3, or .aac).
  2. Open Network Stream: In VLC, click on the Media menu at the top left (or File on Mac) and select Open Network Stream, or simply press Ctrl + N (Cmd + N on Mac).
  3. Paste and Play: Paste the copied URL into the network URL box and click the Play button.

Saving Your Favorite Stations

To avoid entering the URL or searching the directory every time you open VLC, you can save your preferred stations to a local playlist. Once the station is actively playing, go to Media > Save Playlist to File. Save it to your desktop or music folder, and you can simply double-click that playlist file in the future to launch your radio station instantly.
