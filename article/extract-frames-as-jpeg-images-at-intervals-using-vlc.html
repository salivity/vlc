Extract Frames as JPEG Images at Intervals Using VLC

Extracting specific video frames as JPEG images at regular intervals using VLC Media Player is accomplished through the built-in "Scene video filter." This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough on configuring VLC's advanced settings to automatically capture snapshots throughout playback, setting custom frame intervals, choosing the output directory, and saving them directly in JPEG format.

Step 1: Open Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Go to Tools in the top menu and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows / Cmd + , on Mac).
  3. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, look for the Show settings section and select the All radio button to access advanced options.

Step 2: Configure the Scene Filter Settings

  1. In the left-hand navigation pane, scroll down and expand the Video category.
  2. Expand Filters and click on Scene filter.
  3. Set your capture parameters in the right-hand panel:
    • Image format: Enter jpeg (or jpg).
    • Image width: Leave as -1 to preserve the original video width, or enter a specific pixel value.
    • Image height: Leave as -1 to preserve the original video height, or enter a specific pixel value.
    • Filename prefix: Enter a prefix for your images (e.g., frame_).
    • Directory path prefix: Enter the full file path where you want the images saved (e.g., C:\CapturedFrames). Ensure this folder already exists.
    • Recording ratio: This determines the interval. VLC captures one image for every X frames. For example, if your video runs at 30 frames per second (fps) and you want one image every second, set the ratio to 30. If you want an image every 5 seconds on a 30 fps video, set the ratio to 150.

Step 3: Enable the Scene Video Filter

  1. In the left-hand pane, click directly on the parent Filters heading under Video (do not click the arrow next to it).
  2. On the right side, find the list of available video filters.
  3. Check the box next to Scene video filter.
  4. Click Save at the bottom right.

Step 4: Play the Video to Extract Frames

  1. Close VLC completely and reopen it for the settings to take effect.
  2. Open and play the video file you want to extract frames from.
  3. Allow the video to play through the section you need to capture. VLC will silently export JPEG images directly into the specified destination folder at your set interval.

Step 5: Disable the Scene Filter

Once the extraction is complete, make sure to turn off the filter; otherwise, VLC will continue saving snapshots for every video you play.

  1. Go to Tools > Preferences and select All at the bottom left.
  2. Click Video > Filters.
  3. Uncheck the Scene video filter box.
  4. Click Save and restart VLC.
