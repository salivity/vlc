How to Enable Smooth Scrolling in VLC Playlist

This guide explains how to enable smooth scrolling for the VLC media player playlist interface. By default, VLC often scrolls line-by-line using rigid steps, but you can achieve a smoother scrolling experience by configuring VLC's advanced Qt interface preferences and aligning them with your operating system's input settings.

Step 1: Access VLC Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC media player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences, or press Ctrl + P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + , (macOS).
  3. In the bottom-left corner under Show settings, change the selection from Simple to All. This opens the Advanced Preferences menu.

Step 2: Configure the Qt Interface Settings

  1. In the left-hand navigation pane, expand the Interface section.
  2. Expand Main interfaces and select Qt.
  3. Scroll through the right-hand panel to the interaction and playlist options.
  4. Locate the mouse wheel and scrolling behavior settings. Ensure that high-precision scrolling or touchpad gestures are not restricted by fixed-step increments.
  5. If your VLC build provides an option for Pixel-based scrolling or Smooth scrolling, check the box to enable it.
  6. Click Save at the bottom right.
  7. Restart VLC for the changes to take effect.

Step 3: Enable OS-Level Precision Scrolling

VLC relies on the Qt GUI framework, which inherits scrolling behavior directly from your operating system's mouse and touchpad drivers. If the playlist still jumps by rows rather than gliding smoothly:

  • For Windows Precision Touchpads: Open Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad. Ensure scrolling gestures are active; VLC automatically interprets precision touchpad gestures as smooth pixel-by-pixel scrolling.
  • For Standard Mouse Wheels: Traditional mouse wheels send notched "tick" signals that cause VLC to jump multiple lines at once. You can reduce this jumping by going to Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse and reducing the Lines to scroll at a time slider to 1 or 2.
  • For macOS: Open System Settings > Trackpad or Mouse and adjust the Scrolling speed. macOS enables smooth momentum scrolling by default for Qt applications.
