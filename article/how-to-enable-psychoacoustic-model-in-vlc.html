How to Enable Psychoacoustic Model in VLC

This guide provides a straightforward walkthrough for enabling psychoacoustic audio model settings in VLC Media Player. Psychoacoustic modeling optimizes audio performance and compression based on the limits of human hearing perception, typically utilized during encoding or advanced audio processing tasks. Below, you will find the precise steps to navigate VLC's advanced preferences menu, locate the psychoacoustic configuration options, and apply the changes properly.

Step 1: Open Advanced Preferences

  1. Launch VLC Media Player.
  2. Click on Tools in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Preferences from the drop-down menu (or press Ctrl + P on Windows/Linux).
  4. At the bottom-left corner of the Preferences window, locate the Show settings section.
  5. Select the All radio button to switch from simple settings to advanced settings.

Step 2: Navigate to Audio Codec Settings

Psychoacoustic models in VLC are managed primarily through advanced audio codecs (such as Twolame for MPEG Audio Layer II encoding):

  1. In the left-hand navigation pane, scroll down and expand the Input / Codecs category.
  2. Expand the Audio codecs subcategory.
  3. Click on Twolame (or the specific encoder profile you intend to configure).

Step 3: Configure the Psychoacoustic Model

  1. In the right-hand configuration pane, locate the Psychoacoustic model setting.
  2. Change the numerical value according to your requirements:
    • 1: Psychoacoustic model 1 (standard, faster processing).
    • 2: Psychoacoustic model 2 (more complex, higher fidelity calculation).
  3. Adjust any related bitrate or audio quality parameters as required by your project.

Step 4: Save and Apply Changes

  1. Click the Save button in the bottom-right corner of the Preferences window.
  2. Close and restart VLC Media Player to ensure the new psychoacoustic configuration takes effect across future playback and encoding sessions.
