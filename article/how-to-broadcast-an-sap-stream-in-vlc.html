How to Broadcast an SAP Stream in VLC

Broadcasting a Session Announcement Protocol (SAP) stream using VLC media player allows you to multicast media content across a local network while automatically advertising its availability to other clients. By enabling SAP, client instances of VLC can discover and play your broadcast directly from their local playlist menu without requiring manual IP address configuration. The following guide outlines the exact steps to configure and launch an SAP-announced multicast stream in VLC.

Step 1: Open the Stream Wizard

  1. Launch VLC media player.
  2. Click on Media in the top menu bar and select Stream... (or press Ctrl + S on Windows/Linux, Cmd + S on macOS).

Step 2: Select the Media Source

  1. In the Open Media window, choose the tab corresponding to the content you want to broadcast (File, Disc, Network, or Capture Device).
  2. Click Add... to select your file, or configure your device/network URL.
  3. Click the Stream button at the bottom right.

Step 3: Configure Destination and SAP Settings

  1. On the Stream Output setup screen, click Next.
  2. Under the Destination Setup section, locate the New destination dropdown menu.
  3. Select RTP / MPEG Transport Stream (preferred for SAP) and click Add.
  4. In the destination configuration fields:
    • Address: Enter a valid multicast IP address (such as 239.255.1.1 or any address within the 224.0.0.0 to 239.255.255.255 range).
    • Base port: Keep the default port (5004) or set a custom UDP port.
    • Stream name: Enter the name that will appear in the SAP channel list on client players.
    • Group name: (Optional) Assign a category or folder name to group your stream.
  5. Check the box labeled Activate SAP (or SAP Announce).
  6. Click Next.

Step 4: Configure Transcoding and Finalize

  1. In the Transcoding Options section, choose an appropriate encoding profile (such as Video - H.264 + MP3 (TS)) if your source format needs conversion, or leave transcoding unchecked if your media is already multiplexed for transport stream delivery.
  2. Click Next.
  3. Review the generated stream output string in the final screen.
  4. Click Stream to begin the broadcast.

Discovering the Stream on Client Machines

Clients on the same local network subnet can find the broadcast automatically:

  1. Open VLC on the client machine.
  2. Open the Playlist view (Ctrl + L).
  3. In the left sidebar under Local Network, click Network streams (SAP).
  4. The stream name entered during the host configuration will appear in the playlist once the announcement packet is received. Double-click the stream to start playback.
