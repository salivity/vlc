Does VLC Support Amiga MOD Files?

VLC Media Player fully supports the direct playback of Amiga MOD files without requiring third-party plugins or external codecs. Users can simply open these retro audio tracker files natively in VLC across Windows, macOS, and Linux to listen to vintage chiptunes and Amiga computer music directly.

VLC achieves this native playback capability through the integration of the libmodplug decoding library. This library allows VLC to interpret the sequenced note patterns and embedded audio samples contained within standard tracker formats. Alongside the traditional 4-channel and multi-channel Amiga .MOD format, VLC also directly supports other popular tracking extensions such as S3M (Scream Tracker), XM (FastTracker 2), and IT (Impulse Tracker).

To play an Amiga MOD file, you can use any standard media-loading method:

  • Drag and drop the .mod file directly into the open VLC window or playlist.
  • Navigate to the top menu, select Media (or File on macOS), click Open File, and select your tracker file.
  • Right-click the file in your operating system's file browser and choose Open with VLC media player.

While VLC handles the majority of standard Amiga MOD files accurately, it functions purely as an audio player rather than a production environment. Users seeking to view individual sample patterns, manipulate channel panning, or isolate specific instruments may prefer dedicated tracker software like OpenMPT or MilkyTracker. However, for casual listening and broad compatibility, VLC provides immediate, high-fidelity playback of classic Amiga music right out of the box.
