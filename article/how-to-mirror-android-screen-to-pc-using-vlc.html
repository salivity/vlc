How to Mirror Android Screen to PC Using VLC

Yes, you can mirror your Android screen to a PC using VLC Media Player, though VLC functions strictly as the receiver rather than the broadcaster. Because VLC cannot directly capture your phone's display on its own, the process requires an Android app capable of streaming your screen over a local network protocol such as RTSP or HTTP, which VLC on your PC then plays in real time.

How It Works

VLC is designed to play multimedia feeds, including live network streams. By running a screen-casting server on your Android phone, your device generates a local network URL. VLC accesses this URL over your shared Wi-Fi network to display your phone's screen directly on your monitor.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Connect to the Same Network: Ensure your Android smartphone and your PC are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Install a Streaming App on Android: Download an app that streams your screen via HTTP or RTSP (such as "Screen Stream over HTTP" or an RTSP screen broadcaster) from the Google Play Store.
  3. Start the Broadcast: Open the app and tap the option to start the stream. Grant the necessary screen recording permissions when prompted by Android.
  4. Copy the Stream URL: The app will display an internal IP address and port number (for example, http://192.168.1.50:8080).
  5. Open VLC on PC: Launch VLC Media Player on your computer.
  6. Open Network Stream: Click on Media in the top menu bar, then select Open Network Stream (or press Ctrl + N).
  7. Enter the Address: Type or paste the exact URL provided by your Android app into the network URL field.
  8. Play the Stream: Click Play. After a brief buffering period, your Android device's screen will appear in the VLC window.

Limitations to Consider

  • Input Lag: Streaming video over a local network introduces slight latency, making this setup unsuitable for real-time mobile gaming.
  • No Control Input: VLC is only a viewer. You cannot control your Android phone using your PC's mouse or keyboard.
  • Audio Constraints: Depending on the streaming app and your Android version, internal system audio may not transmit along with the video feed.
