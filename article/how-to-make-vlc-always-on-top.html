How to Make VLC Always on Top

Setting VLC Media Player to always stay on top of other desktop windows allows you to watch videos seamlessly while browsing, writing, or working in other applications. This guide provides quick, step-by-step instructions to configure the "always on top" feature both temporarily for a single video session and permanently across all future VLC sessions.

Method 1: Temporary Toggle (Current Session)

If you only need VLC to float above other windows for the duration of a single video, you can toggle the setting instantly:

  1. Open VLC Media Player and start playing your video.
  2. Click View in the top menu bar.
  3. Click Always on top from the drop-down menu.
  4. Alternatively, right-click anywhere inside the video playback area, hover over View, and select Always on top.

A checkmark will appear next to the option indicating it is active. Selecting it again will disable the mode.

Method 2: Permanent Configuration (All Future Sessions)

To ensure VLC automatically launches on top of every window every time you open it, update your application preferences:

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click Tools in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P on Windows).
  3. In the Preferences window, select the Video tab at the top.
  4. Under the Display section, check the box next to Always on top.
  5. Click Save in the bottom-right corner of the window.
  6. Restart VLC Media Player to apply the change permanently.

Once saved, VLC will consistently remain in the foreground above all other running programs whenever media is playing.
