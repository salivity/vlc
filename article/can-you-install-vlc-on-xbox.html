Can You Install VLC on Xbox?

This article provides a direct answer to whether you can install the VLC media player on an Xbox console, outlines the step-by-step installation process, and highlights the key features of the app on the platform. Whether you are using an Xbox One or an Xbox Series X|S, you will learn how to get the app running and what to expect from its performance.

Yes, You Can Install VLC on Xbox

You can absolutely install VLC on your Xbox console. Because the Xbox operating system integrates with the Microsoft Store, the official version of VLC for Windows Store (UWP) is fully compatible with Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles. It is completely free to download and does not require any modifications to your console.

How to Install VLC on Your Xbox

Getting VLC onto your console takes just a few moments through the official storefront.

  1. Turn on your Xbox and ensure you are signed into your Xbox Network account.
  2. Press the Xbox button on your controller and navigate to the Store app (the shopping bag icon).
  3. Select the Search function and type in "VLC".
  4. Choose VLC from the search results (developed by VideoLAN).
  5. Click the Get or Install button. The app will download and install automatically.
  6. Once completed, you can launch VLC directly from the store page or find it under My Games & Apps > Apps.

Key Features and Supported Formats on Xbox

The Xbox version of VLC inherits the robust codec support that made the desktop application famous. It allows you to bypass the file limitations of the native Xbox Media Player.

  • Extensive File Support: It plays almost any video or audio format, including MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, FLAC, and MP3.
  • Subtitles: It supports embedded and external subtitle files (like SRT), allowing you to customize font size and synchronization.
  • Network Streaming: You can stream media directly from local Network Attached Storage (NAS) devices, media servers, or via FTP and UPnP/DLNA shares.
  • Audio Pass-through: It supports advanced audio formats, making it a great addition to a home theater setup.

Are There Any Limitations?

While VLC on Xbox is highly versatile, the Universal Windows Platform (UWP) app does have a few minor limitations compared to the desktop version. It cannot play physical DVD or Blu-ray discs directly through the app; you must use the official Blu-ray Player app provided by Xbox for physical media. Additionally, because it runs on a console operating system, you cannot install custom third-party plugins or skins.
